\subsection{Charter and Trigger Summary}

The Team charter lays out the following expectations:

\begin{itemize}
\item All members in attendance in person for meetings with the supervisor unless an acceptable excuse as listed in the following section is provided
\item Members with information or roles relevant to the meeting agenda should be present for the team’s personal meetings with online or in-person attendance
\item Members are expected to stay till the end of meetings they attend unless significant external circumstances require them leave early. 
\item Members are expected to arrive within 5 minutes of the agreed meeting start time for both personal and supervisor meetings. 
\end{itemize}

An acceptable excuse is one that falls within the following categories:

\begin{itemize}
\item Academic (classes, tutorials, labs)
\item Medical (Major sickness, hospital visit, etc.)
\end{itemize}

In the case of an emergency members are expected to:

\begin{itemize}
\item Let all team members know as soon as possible if you have to miss a meeting and follow-up later to catch up on assigned work / missed discussions 
\item If missing a deadline, let team know as soon as possible and email instructor to explain situation.
\end{itemize}

In addition to these members are expected to maintain the following attitdes and practices:

\begin{itemize}
\item Team members should be respectful of one another.
\item All team members should have a positive attitude and be open to other peoples’ ideas. 
\item Work brought to the team should be in a complete enough state to receive meaningful feedback from another team member.
\item Team members are expected to arrive to meeting having reviewed relevant issues, pull requests, and documents assigned to them beforehand 
\end{itemize}

A violation of any of the previously listed point in this section count trigger a reference of the resolution plan.

\subsection{Resolution Plan}
 When handling major issues, making overarching ddecisions and resolving conflict the team addresses the situation through a majority vote. 
 If the disagreement is major and the team is at a 3 to 2 stand the supervisor's opinion is saught.

 When a charter violation is triggered the team evaluates the violating member, if it is noticed that they have continually failed to meet expectations the issues are escalated to the
 TA or Instructor.
