\subsection{Characteristics of Intended Reader} \label{sec_IntendedReader}

\plt{This section summarizes the skills and knowledge of the readers of the
  SRS.  It does NOT have the same purpose as the ``User Characteristics''
  section (Section~\ref{SecUserCharacteristics}).  The intended readers are the
  people that will read, review and maintain the SRS.  They are the people that
  will conceivably design the software that is intended to meet the
  requirements.  The user, on the other hand, is the person that uses the
  software that is built.  They may never read this SRS document.  Of course,
  the same person could be a ``user'' and an ``intended reader.''}

\plt{The intended reader characteristics should be written as unambiguously and
  as specifically as possible.  Rather than say, the user should have an
  understanding of physics, say what kind of physics and at what level.  For
  instance, is high school physics adequate, or should the reader have had a
  graduate course on advanced quantum mechanics?}

\section{Functional Requirements}
 
\noindent \textbf{Round setup}
\begin{itemize}
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_StartRound}:] When the user presses START and no round is in progress, the system shall begin a round and display the 512 cells with their numbers hidden, arranged as slices of the round's plane colour.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_SelectPlane}:] The system shall randomly select each round's plane colour from yellow (x and y axes continuous), green (x and z axes continuous), or blue (y and z axes continuous). \newline \textit{Rationale:} The two continuous axes form the ``continuous plane'' that the task definitions refer to.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_SelectOrientation}:] The system shall randomly select one of the 384 cube orientations for each round.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_ChooseBase}:] The system shall require the user to choose decimal or octal as the scoring base before the starter appears.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_ShowStarter}:] When the user presses START after choosing the scoring base, the system shall display a randomly chosen starter number in its cell with a white background and start a timer that shows the elapsed seconds.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_NoRepeatStarter}:] The system shall not reuse a starter number for a player until 192 rounds have been played, after which it shall clear that player's list of used starters. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Keeps rounds different from each other.
 
\end{itemize}
 
\noindent \textbf{Tasks and answer checking}
\begin{itemize}
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_TaskOrder}:] The system shall present the eight tasks in order, starting the next task when the user presses START after completing the previous one, and shall not use the same target cells for two tasks in a round.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_CellFeedback}:] When the user selects a cell while a task awaits cell selections, the system shall turn the cell white if it is one of the task's target cells and red if it is not, with no limit on incorrect selections. The system shall ignore a cell that is already white and any selection made while no selection is expected.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Task1}:] In task 1, the system shall accept as correct the cell whose x, y, and z coordinates each differ from the starter's by 4 (modulo 8). \newline \textit{Rationale:} This is the starter's complement. The two numbers sum to 1001 (octal).
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Task2}:] In task 2, the system shall accept as correct the 3 cells that complete a half-orthogonal containing the starter, lying on either axis of the continuous plane and reaching an outer edge.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Task3}:] In task 3, the system shall accept as correct the 7 cells that complete an orthogonal containing the starter on the axis not in the continuous plane.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Task4}:] In task 4, the system shall accept as correct the 7 cells that complete a diagonal containing the starter and lying on both axes of the continuous plane, whether continuous or split. \newline \textit{Rationale:} A split line needs a row, column, or pillar moved to the opposite side of the cube to appear straight.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Task5}:] In task 5, the system shall accept as correct the 7 cells that complete a diagonal containing the starter that uses one axis of the continuous plane and the axis not in the plane, whether continuous or split.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Task6}:] In task 6, the system shall accept as correct the 7 cells that complete a diagonal containing the starter that uses the other axis of the continuous plane (not the one used in task 5) and the axis not in the plane, whether continuous or split.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Task7}:] In task 7, the system shall accept as correct the 7 cells that complete a triagonal containing the starter, whether continuous or split.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Task8}:] In task 8, the system shall accept as correct the 7 cells that complete a split triagonal containing the starter that differs from the triagonal used in task 7.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_ChooseExtra}:] Before tasks 2 to 8 begin, the system shall require the user to choose how many additional numbers to add (1 to 2 for task 2, 1 to 5 for tasks 3 to 8).
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_AdditionColumn}:] When all cells of the target line are found in tasks 2 to 8, the system shall display the starter and the chosen number of additional numbers from the line in a column for the user to add.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_AdditionCheck}:] When the user presses DONE with an answer in the Addition Box, the system shall, if the sum is incorrect, clear the answer, turn the box red, and let the user try again, and, if correct, show the additional numbers in their cells in white.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_SubtractionPrompt}:] After the target cell is found (task 1) or the addition is correct (tasks 2 to 8), the system shall ask the user to subtract from a constant: 1001 minus the starter for task 1, 2002 minus the sum for task 2, and 4004 minus the sum for tasks 3 to 8 (all octal). \newline \textit{Rationale:} A correct subtraction shows the user found the right line, not just a lucky guess.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_SubtractionCheck}:] When the user presses DONE with an answer in the Subtraction Box, the system shall, if the answer is incorrect, clear it, turn the box red, and let the user try again, and, if correct, reset the box to white, show the remaining numbers of the line in their white cells (for task 1, show the answer in the complementary cell), and stop the timer.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_InputValidation}:] If the user presses DONE with an empty answer box, or enters anything other than the octal digits 0 to 7, the system shall show a message asking for a valid answer and shall not count it as an incorrect answer.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_UnrecognizedInput}:] If the user performs an input that the system does not recognize (for example, a key that has no function in the current state), the system shall show a message saying that the input was not recognized. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Tells users why nothing happened instead of leaving them stuck (Hazard Analysis SR17).
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_NextTask}:] When a new task begins, the system shall return the previous task's white cells (other than the starter) to their original colour, clear the previous question and answers, and resume the timer from its previous value.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_EndRound}:] After the subtraction in task 8 is answered correctly, the system shall end the round, display the final score, and allow a new round to begin.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_VerifyTask}:] Before presenting each task, the system shall check that the starter and the task's target cells add up to the correct sum (1001 octal for task 1, 2002 for task 2, and 4004 for tasks 3 to 8) and that the expected answers follow from them, and shall generate a new setup and log the failure if a check fails. \newline \textit{Rationale:} A wrong expected answer would mark correct answers wrong and teach incorrect arithmetic (Hazard Analysis SR9, SR12).
 
\end{itemize}
 
\noindent \textbf{Scoring and controls}
\begin{itemize}
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_BasicPoints}:] When a task's subtraction is answered correctly, the system shall award the line points for the chosen scoring base, and shall award points for each additional number when the addition is correct, as follows (decimal / octal): task 1, 10 / 14; task 2, 20 / 30; tasks 3 and 4, 30 / 44; tasks 5 and 6, 40 / 60; tasks 7 and 8, 60 / 110; each additional number in tasks 2 to 8, 10 / 14. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Octal points are 20\% above decimal points after conversion, as in the rules.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_TimedScore}:] The system shall compute each task's timed score as the task's basic score $\times$ 1350 / $t$, where $t$ is the seconds spent on that task, rounded to the nearest whole number. \newline \textit{Rationale:} The rules give the constant as $2.63671875 \times 512$, which equals 1350. Faster completion scores more. Per-task time and the rounding are our assumptions.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Totals}:] After each task, the system shall display the user's updated total score, and after task 8 the final score, which is the sum of the eight timed scores.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_StopGame}:] When the user presses STOP GAME during a round, the system shall end the round, erase all progress in it, record no score, and return to its ready state.
 
\end{itemize}
 
\noindent \textbf{Saving and recovery}
\begin{itemize}
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Autosave}:] The system shall automatically save the in-progress round each time its state changes, retrying up to 3 times if a save fails and telling the user if all attempts fail. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Prevents lost progress after a crash (Hazard Analysis SR5, SR6).
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Resume}:] When the user returns after the page was closed, reloaded, or crashed during a round, the system shall check that the saved round is valid and offer to resume it with the timer at its saved value, discarding it and telling the user if it is invalid.
 
\end{itemize}
 
\noindent \textbf{3D visualization}
\begin{itemize}
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_3DView}:] The system shall provide a 3D view of the cube as an alternative to the slice display, with each number in the same cell as in the slice display, and shall let the user switch between views at any time without changing the round's state or pausing the timer. Cell selection in the 3D view shall behave as in \rref{FR_CellFeedback}. \newline \textit{Rationale:} The 3D view is an added feature that helps users see how the cube is built (Problem Statement Goal 1). Inner cells are reached by isolating layers (\rref{FR_3DLayers}).
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_3DNavigate}:] In the 3D view, the system shall let the user rotate and zoom the cube using either the pointer or the keyboard.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_3DLayers}:] In the 3D view, the system shall let the user choose any single layer along any axis and show it with the other layers faded or hidden, step to the adjacent layer, and restore all layers.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_3DHighlight}:] The system shall highlight the chosen layer and the cells the user has selected, using visibly different highlights, and shall keep the cell states and highlighting consistent with the round's current state in both views.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_PerformanceWarning}:] If the system detects that the 3D view is running below the minimum performance level set in the Non-Functional Requirements, the system shall warn the user and offer to switch to the slice display. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Gives users with slower devices a way to fix performance problems (Hazard Analysis SR14).

\end{itemize}
 
\noindent \textbf{Guided learning and tutorial}
\begin{itemize}
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Tutorial}:] The system shall provide an interactive tutorial that walks the user through a fixed sample round, requiring them to practice each of the eight task types, and shall let the user skip, exit, or replay it at any time. The tutorial shall show only instructions that match the step the user has reached, and its results shall not be recorded in statistics or leaderboards.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_ExplainCell}:] After an incorrect cell selection, the system shall display a message naming the type of line being sought and the rule it follows, without revealing the correct cell.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Hint}:] When the user requests a hint, the system shall display the coordinate step that the line in the current task follows (for example, ``each coordinate differs by 4'') without identifying a cell, and shall record the request.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_SolutionPath}:] After the user has made 3 incorrect attempts at the same step (3 incorrect cell selections in a task, or 3 incorrect answers to the same question), the system shall let the user request the solution to that step and shall record the request. For cell selection, the solution is shown by highlighting the correct cells one after another in the order the line is traced; for an addition or subtraction, it is shown as the worked octal calculation. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Showing the answer only after repeated attempts keeps the task a challenge.
 
\end{itemize}
 
\noindent \textbf{Accounts, statistics, and competitive play}
\begin{itemize}
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_GuestPlay}:] The system shall let a user play without an account, and shall not send any data about a guest's play online.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_CreateAccount}:] The system shall let a user create an account with a unique username, a unique email address in a valid format, and a password of at least 8 characters, shall reject anything else with a message giving the reason, and shall show the entered username and email address and ask the user to confirm them before the account is created.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_UsernameRules}:] The system shall reject any username shorter than 3 or longer than 20 characters, or that contains a term on a prohibited-terms list. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Usernames appear on public leaderboards, so inappropriate names could harm other users (Hazard Analysis SR13).
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Login}:] The system shall let a user log in and log out, and shall reject invalid credentials with a message that does not say whether the username or the password was wrong. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Avoids revealing which usernames exist.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_PasswordReset}:] The system shall let a user who has forgotten their password request a reset link sent to their registered email address, without revealing whether the address is registered. \newline \textit{Rationale:} The Hazard Analysis lists email as stored user data, and account recovery is its purpose.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_RecordRound}:] When a logged-in user completes a round, the system shall record the final score, scoring base, total time, and date.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Statistics}:] The system shall let a logged-in user view their number of completed rounds, highest score, average score, and fastest round time, for each scoring base.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_Leaderboards}:] The system shall display, for each scoring base, leaderboards of the 10 highest final scores and the 10 fastest round times, showing username and score or time only. A score recorded by the system shall appear on the relevant leaderboards within 1 minute. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Separate boards by scoring base because octal scoring gives 20\% more points.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_LeaderboardIntegrity}:] The system shall create or change leaderboard and statistics records only from rounds it has completed, and shall reject any other request to do so, except for deletion under \rref{FR_DeleteAccount}. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Prevents tampering with rankings (Hazard Analysis SR10).
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_DailyChallenge}:] The system shall offer one daily challenge each day, with the same plane colour, orientation, and starter for every user, and a leaderboard of that day's results.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_ShareResults}:] After completing a daily challenge, the system shall let the user copy a summary of the date, final score, and total time that contains no cell number or answer. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Lets users compare results without spoiling the challenge for others.
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_DeleteAccount}:] The system shall let a logged-in user delete their account, which removes their username, email, credentials, scores, statistics, and leaderboard entries.
 
\end{itemize}
 
\noindent \textbf{Wellbeing}
\begin{itemize}
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_PlayTimeReminder}:] After every hour of play in a session, the system shall remind the user how long they have been playing, without changing the round's state or the timer. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Reduces the risk of excessive use (Hazard Analysis SR21).
 
\item[FR\refstepcounter{reqnum}\thereqnum \label{FR_DisableAnimations}:] The system shall let the user turn off animations, including the rotation animation in the 3D view and visual effects on cell feedback, and shall apply the choice immediately. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Reduces the risk of photosensitive seizures and eye strain from excessive or unintended flashing or animation (Hazard Analysis SR19).
 
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Privacy and Data Retention}
 
\noindent \textbf{PR1:} The system shall store only the data in the data dictionary, and shall not collect real names or birthdates. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Intended users include high-school students, so collecting less personal data means less harm from a leak.
 
\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{PR2:} The system shall use a user's email address only for account recovery (\rref{FR_PasswordReset}), and shall not display it or share it with any third party.
 
\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{PR3:} The system shall store passwords only as cryptographic hashes. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Hazard Analysis SR2. A leak must not expose passwords that users may reuse elsewhere.
 
\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{PR4:} The system shall encrypt email addresses and other sensitive user data in storage. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Hazard Analysis SR1.
 
\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{PR5:} The system shall check that the stored cube array contains each of the numbers 1 to 512 exactly once, and shall not start a round if it does not. \newline \textit{Rationale:} A corrupted array would produce wrong expected answers (\rref{FR_VerifyTask}).
 
\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{PR6:} The system shall automatically back up each database every 24 hours and keep the 7 most recent backups. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Hazard Analysis SR3. The interval and count are proposed values.
 
\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{PR7:} The system shall log every change to each database with the time, operation, and record changed. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Hazard Analysis SR4.
 
\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{PR8:} If a database is corrupted or data is lost, the system shall restore it from the most recent backup that passes an integrity check. \newline \textit{Rationale:} Hazard Analysis SR11.
 
\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{PR9:} The system shall keep a user's scores and statistics until the account is deleted (\rref{FR_DeleteAccount}), and shall then remove the user's data from live databases immediately and from backups when those backups expire.
 
\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent \textbf{PR10:} The system shall not keep any online data from guest play (\rref{FR_GuestPlay}), and shall delete a locally saved round when the round is completed or stopped (\rref{FR_StopGame}).
